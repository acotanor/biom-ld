# Definition
Operational taxonomic units (OTUs) are defined as clusters of highly similar nucleotide sequences that likely represent one or a few closely-related organisms, typically identified by a high degree of nucleotide identity (usually >97%). This clustering helps to manage large datasets by reducing them to representative consensus sequences and counts for subsequent data analysis.
